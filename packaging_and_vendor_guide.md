## 2. Store Naming Strategies (Mubashir & Hamza)
World-famous luxury watch houses are traditionally named after their co-founders (e.g., *Patek & Philippe*, *Audemars & Piguet*, *Vacheron & Constantin*).

Here are the top 4 brand identities tailored for **Muhammad Mubashir** & **Hamza Javed**:

| Option | Brand Name | Brand Slogan | Why it Converts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option 1 (Recommended)** | **M&J CHRONOS** | *Haute Horlogerie* | Timeless, punchy, rolls off the tongue. The "M&J" logo monogram looks royal on watch boxes and cards. |
| **Option 2** | **MUBASHIR & JAVED (M&J Atelier)** | *Geneva Heritage & Precision* | Sounds like an authentic bespoke heritage house. High trust factor for Pakistani customers. |
| **Option 3** | **CHRONO NOIR** *(By M&J)* | *Masters of Time* | Dark, stealth, modern luxury. Appeals heavily to Gen-Z and young corporate buyers. |
| **Option 4** | **VERITAS HOROLOGY** | *Elegance Redefined* | Clean European minimalist aesthetic (like Daniel Wellington / MVMT). |

### How to Brand & Label Without Huge Factory MOQs:
1. **The Box & Card (100% Custom)**: Print your brand name (**M&J CHRONOS**) in gold foil on the box and on the warranty card.
2. **Transparent Caseback Hologram**: Order 500 small circular metallic security hologram stickers with "M&J Authenticity Guaranteed" (costs Rs. 2 each in Urdu Bazaar/Shah Alam). Stick this on the back of every watch.
3. **The Dial**: Leave the factory homage branding (e.g. Poedagar, Curren, Nibosi) as the "Model Series", while your store is the official house (e.g. *M&J Curren Edition*).

## 4. Lahore Wholesale Market Physical Visiting Guide

### A. Exactly Where to Go (Shah Alam & Rang Mahal)
When you visit tomorrow or the day after:

1. **Market Hub 1: Ganpat Road & Rang Mahal Watch Market**
   - **Landmark**: Enter Shah Alam Market from Circular Road / Delhi Gate side, head towards Chowk Rang Mahal.
   - **Key Plazas**:
     - **Madina Watch Market** (Main Rang Mahal Chowk): Packed with watch dealers & Chinese importer basements.
     - **Ganpat Road Watch Market** (Right off Rang Mahal): Direct importers for Curren, Poedagar, Naviforce, Skmei, Nibosi, Lige.
     - **TJ Wholesale (Physical Setup / Shah Alam)**: One of the biggest distributors of wholesale watches and gadgets in Lahore. You can check their online portal (`tjwholesale.pk`) beforehand to verify current inventory and pricing.
2. **Market Hub 2: Rang Mahal & Sarafa Bazaar (For Luxury Boxes & Printing)**
   - **Where**: Walk 2 minutes from Rang Mahal Chowk into Sarafa Bazaar.
   - **Key Shops**: **HJB (Haji Jewel Boxes)** and the entire row of box manufacturers.
   - **What to do**: Ask for "Watch Slide Box with Velvet Cushion" and ask for sample gold foil printing with your name **M&J Chronos**.

### B. Pro-Tips For Visiting The Market:
1. **Never ask for "1 Piece"**: If you ask *"bhai yeh aik piece kitne ka hai?"*, they will quote retail price (Rs. 3,500 instead of Rs. 1,800). Always say:
   > *"Hum online store ke liye 5-6 sample designs le rahe hain. Jo design humari ad campaign me hit hoga, hum direct 50–100 pieces ka carton uthayenge. Saare designs ka wholesale rate batayein."*
2. **Test Before Buying**:
   - Check if chronograph pushers actually move the sub-dials (avoid fake dummy dials!).
   - Check the weight (stainless steel feels heavy and cold to the touch; cheap alloy feels light).
3. **Check Live Wholesale Rates Online Before You Go**:
   - Install **Markaz App** or browse **tjwholesale.pk** on your phone. Search "Poedagar" and "Curren". You will see direct wholesale rates ranging from Rs. 1,200 to Rs. 2,300, so you will already know the exact baseline before walking into the shop!
