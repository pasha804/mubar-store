# Return and Refund Policy — M&J CHRONOS

**Last updated:** October 2026

At **M&J CHRONOS**, we take immense pride in our horological craftsmanship and the rigorous quality control of every timepiece we assemble and dispatch. We want you to purchase with complete peace of mind, which is why we offer Pakistan's most customer-friendly return and inspection policy.

---

### 1. 100% "Open Parcel" Inspection Guarantee (At Your Doorstep)
We understand the hesitation associated with online shopping in Pakistan. Therefore, all orders dispatched through our official courier partners (**Trax Logistics, PostEx, and Leopards Courier**) are sent with an **"Open Parcel Inspection"** authorization.
* When the delivery rider arrives at your doorstep, you have the right to open the flyer and inspect the watch presentation box and physical condition of the watch **before** handing over the cash payment.
* If the timepiece is visibly damaged in transit or incorrect, you may hand the parcel back to the rider without making any payment.

---

### 2. 7-Day Hassle-Free Exchange & Replacement Window
If you have received and accepted your watch and notice any manufacturing defect or movement irregularity, you are entitled to a **free replacement or exchange within 7 days** of delivery.

**To be eligible for an exchange:**
1. The timepiece must be in its original, unworn condition with the protective transparent plastic seals intact on the crystal and caseback.
2. The watch must be in its original **1200 GSM M&J Chronos slide presentation box**, complete with the velvet cushion, warranty card, and authenticity envelope.
3. Proof of purchase (Order Reference ID or registered WhatsApp number) must be provided.

---

### 3. The 1-Year Movement Warranty Card
Every M&J Chronos watch includes a heavy metallic **1-Year Movement Warranty Card** inside the box.
* **What is covered:** Internal mechanical movement failures, quartz movement timekeeping irregularities, and manufacturer defects occurring under normal use for 12 months from the date of purchase.
* **What is not covered:** Normal wear and tear, accidental drops, cracked crystal glass due to impact, water immersion beyond the specified ATM rating, or unauthorized tampering/modifications by third-party repair shops.
* **Claiming Warranty:** Message our WhatsApp Concierge at `+92 300 0000000` with a photo of your warranty card and a short video showing the issue. We arrange pickup and repair or replacement.

---

### 4. Refunds & Reversals
If an eligible replacement unit is out of stock or cannot be provided, a full refund of the product purchase price will be issued:
* **Refund Method:** Direct transfer to your **JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or Pakistani Bank Account**.
* **Processing Timeline:** Refunds are processed within **48 to 72 business hours** following our inspection and receipt of the returned item at our Lahore fulfillment hub.
