# Privacy Policy — M&J CHRONOS

**Last updated:** October 2026

**M&J CHRONOS** ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by founders Muhammad Mubashir & Hamza Javed based in Lahore, Pakistan, respects your privacy and is deeply committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when you browse our store, communicate with our concierge, or place an order.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you interact with our website or place an order, we collect:
* **Contact & Delivery Information:** Your full name, mobile / WhatsApp phone number, city, shipping address, and delivery instructions.
* **Order History:** Details of timepieces purchased, order reference numbers, and exchange records.
* **Device & Technical Information:** Your IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction data via cookies to improve site performance and checkout speed.

*Note:* Because the vast majority of our transactions are Cash on Delivery (COD), we **do not** collect or store sensitive credit card numbers or banking PINs on our servers.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
1. **Fulfill & Deliver Orders:** Dispatch your watch via our courier partners (Trax, PostEx, Leopards) and notify you of dispatch and delivery timelines via WhatsApp / SMS.
2. **Order Verification:** Contact you via WhatsApp or telephone to confirm delivery details prior to dispatch, preventing courier delivery failures (RTO).
3. **Customer Support & Warranty:** Track warranty claims under your 1-Year Movement Warranty Card.
4. **Platform Security & Meta Advertising:** Measure website performance and display relevant timepiece promotions through Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) and Google.

---

### 3. Sharing Your Information
We never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data strictly with trusted service providers necessary for operating our business:
* **Logistics & Courier Services:** Trax, PostEx, and Leopards Courier receive your name, address, and phone number solely for delivery.
* **Shopify & Cloud Infrastructure:** Our hosting and secure e-commerce backend platform.

---

### 4. Data Security
We implement high-grade SSL encryption and security protocols to safeguard your personal details. Access to customer shipping information is strictly restricted to authorized fulfillment staff.

---

### 5. Your Rights & Contact Details
You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you or request that we update or delete your information from our records.
