# Shipping Policy — M&J CHRONOS

**Last updated:** October 2026

**M&J CHRONOS** offers rapid, secure, and insured nationwide delivery across Pakistan. We partner exclusively with top-tier courier logistics networks (**Trax Logistics, PostEx, and Leopards Courier**) to ensure your luxury presentation box arrives in pristine condition.

---

### 1. Shipping Rates & Promotional Free Delivery
* **Nationwide Standard Delivery:** **FREE** (Currently complimentary across all cities, towns, and districts in Pakistan during our promotional period).
* **Cash on Delivery (COD) Surcharge:** **Rs. 0 (FREE)**. We do not charge extra payment collection fees.

---

### 2. Delivery Timelines
All orders are processed and packed within **24 hours** from our central fulfillment hub in Lahore:

| Destination Region | Estimated Delivery Window | Courier Network |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lahore (Local Express)** | **24 to 48 Hours** | Trax / Direct Rider |
| **Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad** | **2 to 3 Business Days** | Trax Logistics / PostEx |
| **Multan, Sialkot, Gujranwala, Peshawar, Quetta** | **3 to 4 Business Days** | Trax / Leopards Courier |

---

### 3. Open Parcel Inspection Authorization
Every shipment sent by M&J Chronos contains an explicit **"Open Parcel Inspection"** sticker on the outer flyer:
1. When the delivery rider arrives, you may open the outer shipping flyer in their presence.
2. Inspect the **1200 GSM M&J Chronos presentation box**, verify the watch model, and ensure there is no transit damage.
3. Hand the cash to the rider once you are fully satisfied.

---

### 4. Shipment Tracking
As soon as your parcel is scanned by the courier, you will receive an automated tracking link and tracking tracking code (CN number) via **SMS and WhatsApp**. You can track the live vehicle status of your parcel directly through the courier's tracking portal.
