### C. The Small MOQ Secret (How to get 4–6 piece packs at wholesale rates)
Wholesalers in Shah Alam are looking for regular bulk buyers. If you go in asking for "1 piece", they give you retail price (+50%).
**The Winning Script to Use:**
> *"Bhai humara digital brand launch ho raha hai Shopify aur Instagram pe. Hum ad testing aur photoshoot ke liye 4 se 6 different designs ke sample mangwa rahe hain. Jis design ka response best aayega, uska direct 50 se 100 units ka box order aayega. Is sample lot pe aap humein transparent wholesale rate dein taake hum regular partner banein."*

They will charge you exact wholesale price or at most PKR 50-100 extra per unit, allowing you to test with minimal capital.

## 5. High-Converting Sales Copywriting & Local COD Strategy
To win in Pakistan, your website must eliminate the customer's fear of online fraud:

1. **"Parcel Khol Kar Check Karein" (Open Parcel Policy)**:
   - Feature prominent badges: *"100% Open Parcel Allowed before payment"* (Coordinate with Trax/PostEx).
2. **1-Year Movement Warranty Card**:
   - Every watch ships with an embossed black metal/gold warranty card. This costs PKR 20 but removes 90% of purchase hesitation.
3. **Sticky Buy Now / Quick COD Form**:
   - Instead of 4-step Shopify checkouts, a 1-click modal asking only: **Name, Mobile Number, City, Full Address**.
4. **Real Customer Unboxing Videos**:
   - Short 9:16 vertical video carousel right under the product gallery.
